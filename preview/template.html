Every species

All 55, drawn from each one's own colour and silhouette archetype — the same eight archetypes the game assembles out of primitives, so no fish needs an uploaded model. Switch the mutation to see what the rare rolls repaint: Golden turns the whole fish to gold metal, Void makes it something that should not exist.

The two ladders

Fish rarity and mutation roll independently, which is why a Glitched Minnow beats most of the Abyss. "Effective" is the real chance once weights are normalised across the tiers a zone can actually produce — the headline 1-in-N is the flavour number.
