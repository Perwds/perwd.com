# perwd.com

## Blockwest

A browser game that mashes up Minecraft and Red Dead Redemption 2: a blocky voxel
frontier with mining and building, a western town, a horse you can whistle for and
ride, a six-shooter with Dead Eye, outlaw camps with bounties, dynamite, and gold to
sell at the general store.

Open `index.html` in a browser (it loads three.js and fonts from a CDN).

**Controls:** WASD move · Shift sprint/gallop · Space jump · Left click shoot/mine/throw ·
Right click aim/place · 1–9 hotbar · Q Dead Eye · E ride/trade/sleep · H whistle ·
R reload · Tab satchel · Esc pause. Phones get on-screen touch controls.

Fan-made and not affiliated with Mojang, Microsoft or Rockstar Games.
